# Version badge opens the update check

## Why this release

- Checking for updates required opening the menu, then **Help & Support**, then **Check for updates**. The version badge in the top bar is visible on every page and is the natural place to check.

## What changed

### Every page

- Double-clicking the version badge in the top bar opens the **LensLedger updates** panel and starts a check for a new release immediately.
- With the badge focused using the keyboard, pressing Enter or Space does the same.
- The badge shows a pointer cursor and a "Double-click to check for updates" tooltip.

### When an update is available

- The badge shows a green dot and a green outline.
- The tooltip names the new version.
- A single click opens the **LensLedger updates** panel, which lists the release notes for the new version and offers **Download, install & restart**. Double-clicking still runs a fresh check.

### Pages other than the home page

- The updates panel lives on the home page, so clicking the badge on Settings, Scan photos, Publish photos and the other pages opens the home page with the panel already showing. A double-click also starts the check there.
- The menu entries **Help & Support > Check for updates** and **About** are unchanged.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 722 tests, all passing.
- Browser check of the badge in the light and dark themes, including the update-available appearance, and of the double-click on the home page and on the Settings page.
