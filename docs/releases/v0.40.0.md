LensLedger's visual identity previously used bright red and orange
colors with a cold blue-gray UI. The new direction is vintage and
retro — muted earth tones, warm browns, and aged textures that feel
like a photographer's darkroom and old paper prints.

## What changed

### New logo

Replaced the bright red/orange chroma logo with the monochrome
vintage variant (`lensledger-logo-chroma-bw.png`). The same camera
shutter and index card design, rendered in muted warm grays and
dark brown. The original logo is preserved as
`lensledger-logo-original.png`.

### Dark theme — "Darkroom"

The dark palette shifted from cold navy/blue-gray to warm
espresso/leather tones:

- **Backgrounds**: deep espresso (#14110e), dark walnut surfaces
  (#201c16, #2a2520), near-black stage (#0c0a08)
- **Text**: aged cream/parchment (#e8dfd0) instead of blue-white,
  warm tan secondary (#a89880)
- **Accent**: antique brass (#c89848) and warm amber instead of
  bright orange
- **Borders**: warm bronze (#3a322a) instead of blue-gray
- **Buttons**: antique brass primary (#a87830), warm charcoal
  secondary
- **Chips/badges**: warm sepia and amber tones
- **Selection**: golden (#d4a840)
- **Map markers**: brass/amber tones matching the accent

### Light theme — "Parchment"

The light palette shifted from cool gray/white to aged paper:

- **Backgrounds**: warm sand (#ddd6c8), aged parchment surfaces
  (#f0e8da, #e0d8c8), dark chocolate stage (#2a2520)
- **Text**: dark chocolate (#2a2420), warm brown secondary (#685848)
- **Accent**: antique gold (#907030) — rich but subdued
- **Borders**: warm tan (#c4b8a0)
- **Header**: cream (#ede5d6)
- **Filmstrip**: warm medium tan (#ccc4b0)
- **Badges**: golden parchment with antique gold borders

### Design philosophy

Both themes draw from the same palette family — warm browns, tans,
ambers, and golds — so switching between them feels cohesive rather
than jarring. The vintage feel complements the monochrome logo and
reinforces LensLedger's identity as a tool for photographers who
care about their image collections.
