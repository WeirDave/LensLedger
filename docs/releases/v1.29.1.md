# Clearer side panel actions and larger touch targets

## Why this release

- Every button in the photo viewer's side panel had the same amber colour, so the one that publishes was no more prominent than **Add tags**.
- The publish section's heading was about twice the size of the other section headings.
- **Trash this photo** sat in the middle of the side panel, between Publish and Capture details.
- Buttons on touch screens were 26 to 38 pixels tall, and the help buttons were 16 pixels.
- Opening **Filters** on a phone put Sort, Rating and the day buttons on rows that split them apart.

## What changed

### Side panel

- **Save subject**, **Add tags** and **Add to event** use the grey secondary button colour. **Preview & publish** is the only amber button.
- The "Publish to this photo" heading uses the same size and style as the other section headings.
- **Trash this photo** is at the bottom of the side panel, below Capture details and Hidden tags. It is an outlined red button instead of a solid one. It is still confirmed and undone the same way.

### Touch screens and narrow windows

- At 768 pixels wide and below, and on devices with a touch screen, the search, scope, date, sort and rating fields, **View**, **Filters**, **Day** buttons, the menu, theme and photo details buttons, and the side panel's buttons and fields are at least 44 pixels tall. The rating stars are 40 pixels. The keyboard shortcut hint is hidden.
- The "i" help buttons are 24 pixels on every screen size.
- With **Filters** open on a phone, the rows are: search and **Filters**; Search scope; **Day**, the date and **Day**; Sort, Rating and **View**.
- The header on a phone is 123 pixels high with **Filters** closed, because of the larger buttons.

### Motion

- When the system's reduced-motion setting is on, the side panel, the menu and the About dialog appear without sliding or fading.

## Not changed

- Publishing, trash, undo, keyboard shortcuts and the order of the other side panel sections.
- The side panel's scroll position: Preview & publish still needs scrolling on shorter windows.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 542 tests, all passing.
- Browser check in light and dark themes at 1024 pixels wide: the section headings match, only **Preview & publish** is amber, and **Trash this photo** is at the bottom of the panel in both themes.
- At 375 by 812 with **Filters** open: no horizontal overflow, the date field is 208 pixels wide, Sort and Rating are 141 pixels each, the menu, theme, **Filters**, **Day**, **View** and photo details buttons are 44 pixels tall, the stars are 40 pixels, and the shortcut hint is hidden.
