# Filmstrip arrows are larger and solid

## Why this release

- The filmstrip's left and right arrow buttons were small and partly see-through, so they were hard to pick out against the thumbnails.

## What changed

### Photo viewer filmstrip

- The arrow buttons are 52 px circles, up from a narrow 36 px strip, and fully opaque.
- On the dark theme they have a light fill with a dark arrow. On the light theme they have a dark fill with a light arrow.
- They are centred vertically on the filmstrip and have a drop shadow. They turn gold on hover and on keyboard focus.
- Behaviour is unchanged: the left arrow is hidden at the start of the strip, the right arrow is hidden at the end once every photo has loaded, and neither shows in the results grid.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 726 tests, all passing.
- Browser check of the arrows in the light and dark themes.
