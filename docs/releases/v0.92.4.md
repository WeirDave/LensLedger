## Motivation

User feedback on the v0.92.0 group manager redesign identified three issues:

1. When a group is selected, only the small badge pill highlighted — you couldn't quickly scan which people belong to a group without reading each badge.
2. The 48px circular avatar thumbnails were too small to identify anyone unless the photo was a close-up selfie. Most photos are group shots or concert photos where a tiny circle is illegible.
3. The photo count ("X photos") was irrelevant to group management and wasted space on the cards.
4. The film strip sitting to the right of the letter looked inconsistent — the People Index puts it below.

## What changed

### Full-card group membership highlight

When a group is selected in the sidebar, every person who belongs to that group gets their entire card highlighted with `--accent-deep` background and `--accent` border. This makes group membership scannable at a glance across hundreds of cards.

### Removed avatars and photo count

Cards are now more compact — just the person's name, aliases (if any), and group badges. This lets more cards fit on screen and keeps the focus on what matters for group management.

### Film strip below letter

Letter section headers now stack the film strip image below the large accent letter instead of beside it, matching the visual pattern of the People Index.
