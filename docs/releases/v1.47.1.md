# People links are always restored after a scan

## Why this release

- Since 1.45.0 a completed scan restores missing people links, with a switch under **Settings** that was on by default. The restore only adds links where a photo file names a person, skips anything removed or rejected, and changes no file, so there was no reason to turn it off. The switch and the setting behind it were removed.

## What changed

### Settings

- The **People** section, with its **Restore people from photo names after each scan** switch, was removed from **Settings**.

### Scans

- Every completed scan restores missing people links from the names saved in the photo files, as before. A paused scan restores nothing. A person removed from a photo, a match rejected earlier, a name hidden from a photo with the tag's remove button, and photos in the Review Bin are still left alone.
- A setting saved by 1.45.0 or 1.45.1 stays in the settings file and is ignored.

### Manual

- Both manuals no longer mention the switch.

## Verified

- Full test suite: all passing. The tests for the switch were removed with it.
