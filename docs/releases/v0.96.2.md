Refined the globe-to-street-view transition and the return trip, making the multi-level zoom experience smoother in both directions.

## Motivation

After the initial street view feature landed in v0.96.0, two usability issues emerged during testing:

1. The transition from globe to street view happened too quickly — clicking a cluster would jump straight to street view without enough intermediate globe zoom levels. Users wanted to see the globe drill down further before the map switch.
2. Exiting street view returned the globe at camera distance 1.6 (the minimum globe distance), which was so close that the earth's curvature was barely visible and markers were too spread out. The transition felt jarring rather than smooth.

## What changed

### Extra globe zoom level before street view

Lowered the street view transition threshold from 1.7 to 1.3. The cluster click handler now uses the unclamped `rawNext` value (before the `Math.max(MIN_DIST, ...)` clamp) to check against this threshold, so the camera can progress through one additional globe zoom level at close range before triggering the street view transition. This gives a 4-step progression instead of 3.

### Smoother exit transition

Changed `exitStreetView()` to return the globe camera at distance 2.2 instead of the previous `TRANSITION_DIST + 0.3` (1.6). At distance 2.2, the globe shows a comfortable regional view with clear earth curvature, making the transition back to the globe feel natural rather than abrupt.

## Verified

- Test suite: 101 tests passed
- Browser verification (dark mode): progressive zoom through 4 globe levels before street view transition, street view loads with full-viewport tiles, exit via home button returns globe at comfortable altitude with visible earth curvature
- Camera distance confirmed at 2.2 after exit (previously 1.6)
