## Motivation

The polaroid-style avatar frames introduced in v0.93.2 used `transform: rotate(-2deg)` which caused the frames to visually overflow outside the card boundaries, sometimes by a noticeable amount.

## What changed

Removed the rotation transform from `.gm-person-avatar`. The polaroid look (white frame, thicker bottom border, drop shadow) is preserved without the tilt, keeping avatars cleanly contained within their cards.

## Verified

- Test suite: 88 tests passed.
- Visual verification in browser: polaroid frames are fully contained within cards in both light and dark themes.
