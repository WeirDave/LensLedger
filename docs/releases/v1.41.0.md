# Search results fill the screen as a grid of photos

## Why this release

- A search showed its matches only in a single row of small thumbnails along the bottom of the page, with the first match opened in the viewer. Choosing among many matches meant scrolling that row sideways.
- The search toolbar held a dozen controls in one crowded row, and the search scope menu used internal names ("Visible image tags", "Day/event context") that did not say what each option did.

## What changed

### Results grid

- A search, a filter, a date, a rating or a special view now fills the whole area below the toolbar with a grid of large thumbnails of the matching photos. More photos load as the grid is scrolled.
- Choosing a photo in the grid opens it in the viewing screen, with its details in the sidebar. The filmstrip along the bottom of the viewing screen still lists every match, and the arrow keys still step through them.
- **Back to results** (top left of the photo) or the **Esc** key returns to the grid at the same scroll position.
- The home page opens as a grid of the newest photos. A link that names a specific photo, such as one from the photo map, opens that photo directly.
- Ctrl+click and Shift+click still select several photos in the grid for tagging, rating or trashing. The 1–5 rating keys apply only to the selected photos while the grid is showing, and the arrow keys are inactive there.
- In Exact duplicates and Similar photos, each set is separated by a bar across the grid and each photo is labelled with its place in its set.

### Search toolbar

- The search box is wider and sits on its own row with a **Search** button, **Filters** and **Collections**.
- The search scope menu is replaced by buttons under the search box: **Everything** (default), **Photo contents**, **Folder & event** and **Meaning (AI)**. Hovering a button explains it.
- **People**, **Exact duplicates**, **Similar photos** and **Needs tags** are in a **Special views** menu next to those buttons.
- **Sort** is at the right of the same row. Choosing a scope button, a special view or a sort order applies it immediately.
- **Filters** (previously "More filters") now holds every narrowing control in one panel: **Date** with its previous-day and next-day buttons, **Rating**, taken from and until, people, type, shape, location, camera and folder. The button shows how many filters are active.
- The date and the rating now appear as removable buttons under the toolbar alongside the other active filters.
- On a phone the toolbar fits the screen width, and the scope buttons scroll sideways.

### Fixed

- Scrolling for more results while the type, shape, location, camera, folder, date-range or people filters were active loaded further photos without applying those filters. Every page now uses the full set of filters in the address.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 658 tests, all passing.
- New tests cover the page opening as a results grid unless a photo is named, the scope buttons and special views menu, and the date and rating filter buttons.
- Browser check of the grid, the viewing screen, the Filters panel and the toolbar at desktop and phone widths in the light and dark themes, using a generated library.
