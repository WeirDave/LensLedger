## Why this release

The About panel, opened from "About" in the Navigation menu, was laid out
correctly only on the Settings page. On every other page the logo was shown
at full size and overflowed the panel, and the text ran together. Examples
were "Current libraryC:\…" and the "Other projects by the developer" names
running into their descriptions.

Each page's stylesheet carried its own copy of the About panel styles. The
panel itself had been redesigned, with a centred logo, a library box and
project cards, but only the Settings page's copy had been updated. The other
pages still had styles written for the earlier layout.

## What changed

- The About panel styles now live once in the theme stylesheet that every
  page loads. All pages show the same About panel, in light and dark themes.
- The outdated copies were removed from the ten other page stylesheets.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 234 tests, all passing.
- In the browser on a test library: the About panel on Publish photos and
  Manage groups matches Settings, with the logo sized and centred and the
  library box and project cards laid out. Checked in light and dark themes.
