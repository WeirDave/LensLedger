# Year menu for the photo grid

## Why this release

- The home page opens as a grid of the newest photos. Reaching an older year meant scrolling past every newer photo, or typing a date range into the Filters panel.

## What changed

### Home page photo grid

- A **Year** menu sits beside **Sort** in the search toolbar. It lists every year that has photos, newest first, with the number of photos in that year, for example "2019 (1,204)".
- Choosing a year shows only that year's photos in the grid. **All years** returns to the full library.
- The menu sets **Taken from** and **Taken until** under **Filters** to 1 January and 31 December of the chosen year. A single **Date** that was chosen is cleared. The search text, Sort, Rating and the other filters stay as they were.
- The active year appears as one **Year** chip above the grid. Selecting the chip removes it. A date range that is not exactly one calendar year still appears as separate **From** and **To** chips, and the menu shows **All years**.
- The year counts cover the whole library, not only the current search. Photos in the Review Bin and photos without a capture date are not counted.
- The menu is not shown on the People page.
- The user manual and the in-app manual describe the **Year** menu.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 729 tests, all passing.
- Browser check of the Year menu and the Year chip in the light and dark themes, and at phone width.
