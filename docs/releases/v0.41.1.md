Added "Empty trash" for bulk permanent deletion.

## What changed

The Trash & Restore panel now has an "Empty trash" button at the top
when items are present. Clicking it prompts for confirmation, then
permanently deletes all trashed files and removes their associated
data (tags, people, faces) from the database. This mirrors the
familiar "Empty Recycle Bin" pattern from Windows.

A new `/api/review-bin/empty` endpoint handles the bulk deletion
server-side.
