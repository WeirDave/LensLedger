## What changed

The terminal log during scans was too quiet — each phase just said "Scan started" or "OCR started" without identifying which phase was running, and small batches (e.g., 13 new photos) completed before progress thresholds were reached, producing no output at all between "started" and "complete".

Now every scan phase:

1. **Identifies itself clearly** with a consistent prefix:
   - `Photo locations: scanning C:\Dropbox\Photos`
   - `Text recognition (OCR): starting`
   - `Meaning search: starting`
   - `Face detection: starting`

2. **Logs its total count** as soon as it knows how many items it has to process:
   - `Photo locations: ~50,026 files to check`
   - `Text recognition (OCR): 13 images to process`
   - `Meaning search: 13 images to index`
   - `Face detection: 847 photos to scan`

3. **Reports progress at lower intervals** so updates actually appear:
   - Photo locations: every 2,000 files (was 500)
   - OCR: every 25 images (was 100)
   - Meaning search: every 50 images (was 100)
   - Face detection: every 50 photos (was 100)

4. **Completion messages use the same prefix** for consistency:
   - `Photo locations: done — 1 changed, 348 removed, 49,679 total assets`
   - `Face detection: done — 367 faces found in 847 photos`

## Verified

- Test suite: 88 tests passed (Python 3.14, Windows)
