## Motivation

The group sidebar used a megaphone emoji (📢) as the icon for each group, which had no connection to the concept of people groups and was hard to identify at the small size.

## What changed

Replaced the megaphone with the people silhouette emoji (👥) in both the group list items and the empty-state placeholder. This immediately communicates "group of people."
