The photo map previously loaded all location data as fixed clusters
pre-grouped at ~11km resolution on the server. No matter how far you
zoomed in, nearby but distinct locations stayed merged into one dot.
The maximum zoom was only 8x, which wasn't enough to distinguish
locations within a city. This release replaces the static marker
approach with dynamic client-side clustering.

## What changed

### Server: finer-grained location data

The `/api/map/points` endpoint now groups photos at 0.001-degree
resolution (~100m) instead of 0.1 degrees (~11km), and raises the
limit from 5,000 to 50,000 groups. This gives the client much more
granular data to work with at higher zoom levels.

### Client: dynamic clustering

The map JS was rewritten to implement grid-based dynamic clustering:

- All location points are loaded once, then clustered on the client
  based on the current zoom level and a pixel-distance threshold.
- As you zoom in, the grid cell size shrinks and clusters split apart
  into smaller groups or individual markers.
- As you zoom out, nearby markers re-aggregate into larger clusters.
- Re-clustering is debounced (80ms) to keep zoom and pan smooth.

### Click-to-zoom into clusters

Clicking a multi-photo cluster now zooms into it at 2.5x the current
zoom, centering on that cluster's location. The cluster splits apart
at the new zoom level, revealing its constituent locations. Clicking
a single-photo marker still opens the details panel as before.

### Viewport culling

Only markers whose map coordinates fall within (or near) the visible
viewport are considered for clustering and rendering. Points off-screen
are skipped entirely, so panning through a dense area stays responsive
even with thousands of underlying data points.

### Higher zoom range

Maximum zoom increased from 8x to 32x, allowing detailed exploration
of locations within a neighborhood or even a building complex.

### Compact count labels

Clusters with 1,000+ photos now show abbreviated counts (e.g., "2k"
instead of "2000") to keep markers readable.

## Verified

- Test suite: 79 tests, all passing.
- Browser testing: loaded the map with 18,467 located photos across
  3,998 places. At world zoom, clusters display with correct counts.
  Scrolled to zoom in — clusters dynamically split into smaller groups.
  Clicked a multi-photo cluster — map zoomed to 2.5x and re-clustered,
  showing more granular markers. Continued clicking clusters to drill
  down through US → Southwest → individual city-level markers. Clicked
  a single-photo marker — details panel opened with photo preview,
  date, coordinates, and action buttons. Reset map — returned to world
  view with re-clustered markers. No JS console errors from map code.
