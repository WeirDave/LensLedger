# LensLedger v0.64.3

## Clearer face identification workflow guidance

### The problem

The Name faces page, user manual, Quick guide, and face detection completion message didn't explain the recommended two-stage workflow. Users could spend hours naming faces one by one in the Name faces grid without realizing that the much faster path is to name a handful of people there, then let Review people do the bulk identification automatically.

### What changed

**Name faces intro text** — Rewritten to explain upfront: "You don't need to name every face here — just name a handful of different people (5–10 is plenty), confirming the 'Also looks like' matches as they come up. Then head to Review people..." with a direct link.

**User manual (People and Faces section)** — Stage 1 is now subtitled "seed the system" and Stage 2 "the fast part." The manual explicitly says you don't need to work through the entire queue, just name 5–10 people and move on.

**Quick guide** — Added a new paragraph explaining the Name faces → Review people workflow in plain language.

**Face detection completion message** — Changed from "Use 'Name faces' to put names on them, then 'Find more matches' on People review" (confusing — no button called "Find more matches") to "Name a few people in 'Name faces,' then go to 'Review people' where LensLedger will suggest matches across your whole library."

## Verified

- Test suite: 80 tests passed.
- Browser verification: confirmed the new intro text on Name faces page, the new paragraph in the Quick guide modal, and the updated user manual section.
