# Progress shown while naming a face and loading a group

## Why this release

1.24.0 offers faces for review at a lower similarity, so "Also looks like"
groups got much bigger, up to 200 faces. On a real library that exposed a
problem that had always been there but was small:

- Naming a face showed only "Saving…" until the server had searched every
  unnamed face and replied, with nothing to say it was working.
- Then the group filled in slowly. Each face thumbnail makes the server open the
  whole photo and cut out the face, so a group of 200 means 200 photos opened,
  and until each one arrived its square sat black. A page of black squares with
  no counter looks exactly like a hang.

## What changed

### Naming a face says what it is doing

- The card shows a spinner with "Saving the name…". After two seconds it says
  "Looking for faces that look like <name>… 3s" and counts the seconds. After ten
  seconds it adds "still working, checking N unnamed faces", so a long wait on a
  big library is explained.
- The spinner is created once and only the text changes, so it does not restart
  every second.
- A notice that the catalogue is busy (a scan is using it) replaces the text, as
  before. The same status is used when naming from the enlarged view.

### A group says it is loading

- The group's header shows a spinner and "Loading faces… 12 of 200" until every
  thumbnail has arrived, then the line disappears.
- A thumbnail still to come shows a light, moving placeholder instead of a black
  square. One that cannot be loaded shows a "?" and is counted as finished, so the
  counter always reaches the end.
- A group's thumbnails are now requested straight away instead of as they scroll
  into view. The counter needs that to be accurate, and a group is small on screen.

### Thumbnails load about twice as fast

- The face thumbnail address takes an optional `size`, 64 to 360, default 360.
  A JPEG is decoded at 1/2, 1/4 or 1/8 of its size when the face crop is large
  enough to allow it, and never smaller than the thumbnail needs, so the thumbnail
  is not blurrier for it. A rotated photo is handled by swapping the sides asked
  for.
- A group asks for 160 pixels because it shows 74-pixel thumbnails. Face cards,
  the enlarged view and every other page still get 360.

## Measurements

On a generated 12-megapixel JPEG with a face a little over a tenth of its width,
one thumbnail took 209 ms before and 90 ms after, so a 200-face group's thumbnails
would take 42 s one at a time before and 18 s after. The browser asks for several
at once, so the real wait is shorter. The look-alike search behind "Saving…" took
0.3 s warm and 1.4 s cold across 36,000 generated faces. These are timings on
generated data on a Linux server, not on a real library.

## Known limits

- This makes the wait visible and shorter. It does not remove it. A library on a
  slow disk, or photos in a cloud folder that must be downloaded before they can be
  read, will still be slow, and the counter will show it.
- The counter counts thumbnails. The search that happens before the group appears
  is shown by the seconds counter on the card instead.
- Thumbnails are not kept on disk, so reopening a group decodes them again.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 434 tests. 5 of them (publishing tests in `test_server.py`)
  error on the Linux machine this was developed on because the bundled
  `ExifTool.exe` is a Windows program. They error identically on 1.25.0. The other
  429 ran: 399 passed and 30 were skipped.
- New tests build a 1600 by 1200 photo with a red square exactly under the face
  box, upright and stored rotated by its EXIF orientation, and check that a 160
  pixel thumbnail is made from the right place (its centre is red and its padding
  is white), is not smaller than asked, and that `size` is limited to 64 to 360 and
  defaults to 360. Forcing the most aggressive reduction made them fail, with a
  77-pixel thumbnail where 160 was asked for, and restoring it made them pass.
- The People page was driven in Chromium in the light and dark themes against the
  real server, with the naming request and each thumbnail slowed on purpose and one
  thumbnail made to fail: the card read "Saving the name…" then "Looking for faces…
  3s" and, after ten seconds, "still working, checking 12 unnamed faces", with a
  single spinner; the group's counter counted from 0 up to 9 of 10; the failed
  thumbnail showed its "?" and the counter still finished; and the placeholders were
  light in both themes.
