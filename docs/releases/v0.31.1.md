The Scan photos page has two levels of progress: the "Run all scans" card
at the top, which chains photo locations → OCR → meaning search → face
detection, and each individual job's own card further down. Both were
already fed the same `done`/`total` numbers from the running job's status
endpoint, but only the individual cards turned those into a percentage and
an ETA via `progressSuffix()` (`web/js/scan-photos.js`). The "Run all
scans" summary only ever showed `elapsedText()` plus "step N of M" — so
during a long face-detection pass, the one card most people are actually
watching had no percentage on it at all, only a step count.

The fix reuses the exact numbers the summary was already computing for its
own progress bar (`scanAllStepFraction`) — `done`/`total`/`started_at` for
whichever step is currently running — and threads them into the same
`progressSuffix()` call the individual card uses. No new state, no new
polling: the summary line now reads "10h 23m elapsed · step 4 of 4 · 9% ·
~45h 36m remaining" instead of stopping at "step 4 of 4".

Also carried over a small existing gap in `elapsedText()`: elapsed times
past 60 minutes were only ever displayed as a raw minute count (e.g.
"623m 12s elapsed"), with no hour component. It now switches to
`Xh Ym Zs elapsed` past the one-hour mark, matching the format
`formatDuration()` already used for the ETA half of the same line.
