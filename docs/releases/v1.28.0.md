# Review and fix faces marked Unknown person or Not a person

## Why this release

Faces marked **Unknown person** or **Not a person** disappear from the People page and
are excluded from face matching. A face marked by mistake, or one that later turned out
to be a known person, could not be found again or reversed from within LensLedger.

## What changed

- **Set-aside faces** is a new page. It opens from the navigation menu, or from the
  **Set-aside faces** button at the top of the People page. The **Unknown person** and
  **Not a person** tabs list the faces marked each way, with a count on each tab. The
  most recently marked faces come first, and **Show more** loads further pages.
- Each face has **Who is this?** to name it. Naming removes the mark and tags the face
  with the chosen person, the same as naming it on the People page.
- **Put back for review** removes the mark so the face appears on the People page again.
- **Enlarge** shows the full photo with the face outlined. The enlarged view has the
  same **Who is this?** and **Put back for review** controls.
- **Select** at the top of the page turns on selection. With faces selected, **Assign to…**
  names them all as one person and **Put back for review** removes the mark from all of
  them. **Select all shown** and **Clear** change the selection.
- Naming a face that was marked Unknown person or Not a person clears the mark. Before,
  a named face could keep the mark and stay excluded from the suggestions for that person.

## Not changed

- The **Unknown person** and **Not a person** buttons on the People page and on the
  suggestion review page work as before.
- Faces without a recorded face location are not listed, because no face image can be
  shown for them.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 467 tests, all passing, including new tests for the list, the
  restore, naming a marked face and the page.
- Checked in the browser against a generated test library, in light and dark themes: tabs
  with counts, the face grid, selection, and **Put back for review** updating the lists
  and counts.
