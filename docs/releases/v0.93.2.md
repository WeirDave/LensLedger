## Motivation

The circular avatar thumbnails added in v0.93.1 missed an opportunity to lean into the photography theme. Additionally, polaroid frames appeared at slightly inconsistent sizes because the image area wasn't explicitly fixed — source images from `/media-face` varied in aspect ratio (from 82x109 to 360x360), and `nth-child` rotation selectors counted letter header elements as children, creating an uneven rotation pattern.

## What changed

- Restyled `.gm-person-avatar` from a circle to a polaroid-style frame: white background, 3px padding on top/sides, 9px on the bottom (the classic instant-photo look), 2px border-radius, and a subtle drop shadow.
- All polaroids tilt at a consistent -2deg instead of varying by `nth-child` position.
- Fixed the image area to exactly 36x36px with `object-fit: cover`, so every face fills identically regardless of the source crop dimensions.

## Verified

- Test suite: 88 tests passed.
- Visual verification in browser: polaroid frames display uniformly in both light and dark themes, with consistent sizing across all cards.
