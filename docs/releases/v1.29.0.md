# Toolbar fits narrow windows, smaller header, accessibility labels

## Why this release

- In windows between about 950 and 1100 pixels wide, the viewer's toolbar was wider than the window. The theme button, **View** and the right edge of the photo details panel were cut off with no scrollbar.
- At 950 pixels and below, **Sort** and **Rating** were hidden with no other way to reach them.
- The header and the filmstrip row left little height for the photo, most of all on a phone.
- Several controls had no accessible name, and the main buttons and placeholder text were below the contrast needed for comfortable reading.

## What changed

### Photo viewer toolbar

- The toolbar no longer sets the width of the page. In windows wider than 960 pixels it shows every control on one row, and wraps onto a second row when space runs out.
- At 960 pixels wide and below, the toolbar shows the search box and a **Filters** button. **Filters** opens Search scope, **Day**, the date, **Sort**, **Rating** and **View**. The button shows how many filters are in use, for example "Filters · 2" for a rating and a date. No control is removed at any width.
- The Filters count covers date, scope other than "Everything", sort other than "Newest first", and a rating filter.

### Layout

- The header is shorter. The logo is 40 pixels (32 on narrow screens) instead of 60 (48). The header is about 120 pixels high on a desktop window and about 100 pixels on a phone with **Filters** closed.
- On narrow screens the filmstrip row is as tall as its thumbnails instead of a fixed 158 pixels.
- The page height follows the visible screen height on phones, where the browser's toolbar changes it.

### Accessibility

- The previous and next photo buttons are named "Previous photo" and "Next photo".
- The photo has alt text: the photo's description when it has one, otherwise the file name.
- The status line and the message shown after moving a photo to the review bin are announced politely by screen readers.
- Search scope, primary subject, photo tags, event or folder tags and the tags field in the batch bar have names.
- **Filters** and the photo details button report whether they are open.
- On narrow screens the photo details panel cannot be reached with the Tab key while it is closed.
- Focused buttons and fields show a visible outline in both themes.

### Readability

- The main button colour is darker in both themes: the light text on the button now has a contrast of about 4.8 to 1 in both themes, up from 3.6 (dark) and 4.3 (light). This affects every page that uses the main button colour.
- Placeholder text in the viewer uses the secondary text colour instead of the browser's grey.
- The publish note is 12 pixels instead of 11, and filmstrip captions are 11 pixels instead of 10.

## Not changed

- The order and content of the photo details panel, publishing, trash and the keyboard shortcuts.
- Pages other than the photo viewer keep their layouts; only the main button colour is shared with them.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 542 tests, all passing.
- Browser check in light and dark themes at 1024 pixels wide: the page matches the window width, **View**, the theme button and the sidebar buttons are fully on screen, and the header is 120 pixels high (138 before).
- At 800 pixels wide: no horizontal overflow, and **Filters** shows with Sort and Rating inside it.
- At 375 by 812: no horizontal overflow, the header is 102 pixels high with **Filters** closed (222 before), the photo area is 597 pixels high (432 before), the filmstrip row is 113 pixels (158 before), the photo details panel is not focusable while closed and is focusable once opened, and **Filters** shows its count after a rating filter is applied.
