# Small face boxes are easier to see on the enlarged photo

## Why this release

Clicking **Enlarge** on a face on the People page opens the full photo with a "This
face" box around the detected face. When the face is small relative to the photo, the
box was only a few pixels wide and could be mistaken for being missing, which made it
hard to tell what the detector had picked up.

## What changed

- The box is never drawn smaller than 44 pixels. It is grown around the centre of the
  detected face, so it still sits on the same spot.
- A box that had to be enlarged is drawn with a dashed outline and a slow pulse, so a
  small detection stands out from a normal-sized one.
- The same sizing applies to the "Face being checked" box when reviewing a person's
  faces, and it is kept while zooming and panning the photo.

## Not changed

- Boxes for faces that are already larger than 44 pixels are drawn as before.
- Face detection, the stored face boxes and which faces are offered are unchanged.
  Nothing on the server changed.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 463 tests, all passing. The change is in page scripts and
  stylesheets only, so no Python test covers it.
- A synthetic test page using the real `lightbox-zoom.js` and stylesheet was checked in
  the browser: a face about 15 pixels wide is drawn as a 44-pixel dashed, pulsing box
  with its label, and a normal-sized face keeps its exact size, solid outline and
  label. The full People page was not exercised.
