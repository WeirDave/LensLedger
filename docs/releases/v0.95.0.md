The flat equirectangular map projection used for the Photo Map page distorted
landmass shapes and sizes — Greenland appeared as large as Africa, and distances
near the poles were wildly exaggerated. This release replaces it with an
interactive 3D globe that shows the Earth's actual geometry.

## What changed

The Photo Map now renders a Three.js WebGL globe instead of a flat SVG
background with CSS transforms. The existing world-map SVG (coastlines from
TUBS/Wikimedia Commons) is loaded as a texture and wrapped around a sphere,
giving an accurate spherical projection.

**Interaction model**:
- **Drag** rotates the globe (replacing the old pan behavior)
- **Scroll** zooms in and out (same as before)
- **Click a cluster** zooms in and centers on that location
- **Click a small marker** opens the details panel with photo preview, dates,
  coordinates, and links — identical to the old flat map

**Visual enhancements**:
- Subtle atmospheric glow around the globe edges
- Markers smoothly track their positions during rotation
- Front-side visibility culling hides markers on the far side of the globe

**Preserved features**:
- Server-side pre-clustering of nearby coordinates
- Client-side grid-based clustering that adapts to zoom level
- Details panel with photo preview, "Open photo", "View all photos here",
  and "Open in OpenStreetMap" links
- Deep linking via `?lat=...&lon=...` query parameters
- Empty-state overlay when no GPS data exists
- Both light and dark theme support

## Technical details

- Three.js r128 (603 KB) is included locally in `web/js/three-min.js` — no
  CDN dependency, keeping the app fully offline-capable
- The SVG texture is rasterized to a 2048×1024 canvas at load time and applied
  as a `CanvasTexture` to a 96-segment sphere
- Camera uses a perspective projection (45° FOV) with spherical coordinate
  positioning controlled by two angles (latitude/longitude-aligned) plus distance
- Marker positions are projected from 3D sphere coordinates to 2D screen
  coordinates each frame, with full DOM rebuild only when the camera settles
- The `#world` div (old flat map container) is replaced by `#markerLayer`,
  a transparent overlay for HTML marker buttons
