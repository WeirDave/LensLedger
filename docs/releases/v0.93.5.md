## Motivation

Despite removing the rotation in v0.93.3, polaroid avatar images could still overflow their frames — the container had no explicit height or overflow clipping, so images extended past the card boundary.

## What changed

Added `height: 48px` and `overflow: hidden` to `.gm-person-avatar`, ensuring the image is always clipped within the polaroid frame regardless of the source image dimensions.

## Verified

- Test suite: 88 tests passed.
- Visual verification in browser: polaroid frames clip images cleanly in both light and dark themes, no overflow visible.
