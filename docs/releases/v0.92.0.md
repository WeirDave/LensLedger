## Motivation

The bulk group management page shipped in v0.90.0 was functional but visually utilitarian — a flat checkbox list with no photos, no visual hierarchy, and no personality compared to the rest of LensLedger. It needed a complete visual overhaul to match the polished, photo-rich aesthetic of the People Index.

Additionally, with 200+ people in a library, scrolling through an unsorted flat list was slow. The People Index solved this with an alphabet jump bar, and the group manager needed the same.

## What changed

### Photo cards with avatars

Each person now renders as a card with a circular avatar showing their representative photo (using the same COALESCE logic as the People Index — card photo, then newest confirmed, then newest suggested). Cards show the person's name, photo count, aliases, and group badges as styled pills. A check indicator circle on the right fills with the accent color when selected.

### Alphabet jump bar

A sticky A–Z bar sits below the toolbar. Letters with matching people are clickable and scroll the grid to that letter section. Disabled letters (no people starting with that letter) are dimmed. A `#` link covers non-alphabetic names.

### Film strip letter separators

Each letter section begins with a large accent-colored letter and the film strip image, matching the People Index style. The film strip uses `mix-blend-mode: multiply` in light mode and `filter: invert(1); mix-blend-mode: screen` in dark mode.

### Toast notifications

Group operations (create, add, remove, delete) now show a toast notification at the bottom of the screen instead of inline status text.

### Sidebar polish

Group items in the sidebar now include an icon, a styled member count pill, and a hover-reveal delete button. Selected groups highlight with the accent color.

### Responsive grid

The people area uses a CSS grid with `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(260px, 1fr))` that adapts to any viewport width. On mobile (under 700px), the sidebar stacks above the grid.
