## Why this release

The Backups section of the Scan photos page shows a warning when photos were
being written as LensLedger stopped. It listed photos that had in fact been
written successfully, and it offered no way to act on the list other than
opening each photo's own page.

Two kinds of write record whether they finished differently. "Write all
tags" records a write before touching the photo and marks it finished
afterwards, so an unfinished record means the write was cut off. "Publish
people metadata" and publishing a single photo record their write only once
it has finished and been verified, but never marked it finished, so every
photo they wrote was later reported as interrupted. The list was also capped
at 200, so the count stopped there.

## What changed

- Publishing people and publishing a single photo now record their writes as
  finished.
- When the catalog is next opened, existing records left unfinished by those
  two kinds of publish are marked finished. Records left unfinished by
  "Write all tags" are kept, as those writes may really have been cut off.
  The false list therefore clears on its own after updating.
- The warning explains that each photo is written in a single step, so a
  listed photo is either fully written or still as it was, and offers two
  buttons:
  - **"Keep the photos as they are"** clears the warning and leaves the
    photos unchanged.
  - **"Put back the originals"** asks for confirmation, then restores each
    listed photo from the safety copy taken just before its write. Any photo
    that cannot be restored stays on the list with the others cleared.
- The warning shows the full number of photos, lists the first 20 and says
  how many more there are.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 226 tests, all passing, on Python 3.12.10. New tests
  check that a finished single-photo publish is not reported as interrupted,
  that "Keep the photos as they are" clears the list, that "Put back the
  originals" restores the photo's bytes from its safety copy, and that the
  catalog upgrade clears unfinished publish records while keeping an
  unfinished "Write all tags" record.
- Checked in the browser against a generated test library in light and dark
  themes, with a synthetic list of 23 interrupted writes: the heading gave
  the full count, 20 photos were listed with "and 3 more", a photo without a
  safety copy said so, and "Keep the photos as they are" cleared the warning
  and reported the result under the section.
