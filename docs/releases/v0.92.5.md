## Motivation

v0.92.4 had two visual bugs:

1. The in-group card highlight used `--accent-deep` as a background fill, which in light mode is a dark brown (`#604810`) — text was nearly illegible on it.
2. The film strip image inside letter headers had no width constraint and stretched across the full grid column width, warping the image.

## What changed

### Border instead of background fill

Group membership is now indicated by a thick 4px `--accent` border instead of a background color. This works cleanly in both dark and light themes without affecting text readability.

### Film strip sizing

Added `max-width: 200px` and `align-self: start` to the film strip image so it renders at its natural aspect ratio instead of stretching to fill the grid column.

## Verified

- Test suite: 88 tests passed.
- Browser verification: tested in both dark and light themes with "Bright House Networks" group selected.
- Confirmed border highlight is visible and text remains readable in both themes.
- Confirmed film strip renders at natural proportions below each letter.
