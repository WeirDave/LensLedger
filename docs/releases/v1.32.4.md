# Trash confirmation inside the page on the People review pages

## Why this release

- The trash buttons on the People review pages confirmed with the browser's own dialog. A browser that has blocked further dialogs for a page answers such a dialog with Cancel and shows nothing, so the button appeared to do nothing. v1.32.1 fixed this in the photo viewer only.

## What changed

### People review pages

- The trash button on a photo card in the suggestions page and in the unidentified faces page, and the **Trash** button in the enlarged face view, open a "Move to Trash?" panel with **Cancel** and **Move to Trash**. Pressing Escape or clicking outside the panel cancels.
- After **Move to Trash**, the photo leaves the page and the Undo toast appears as before.
- While the panel is open, keyboard shortcuts of the page behind it do not run.

### Not changed

- Prompts on the Scan photos, Settings and group-management pages, and the people merge prompts in the viewer, still use the browser's dialog.

## Verified

- Test suite: 546 tests plus 3 new ones, all passing.
- Browser: the panel was opened on the People page in light and dark themes with a test library. Escape cancels, **Move to Trash** confirms, and the panel closes without the key reaching the page behind it. A phone-width check was not possible in the test browser.
