# LensLedger v0.76.2 — Consistent "People review" naming throughout the app

## Motivation

After consolidating the two-page workflow into "People review" in v0.76.0,
several user-facing references still said "Name faces" or "Review people":
the Quick guide, Scan photos metric labels, the user manual, a page title,
and a button on the People Review completion screen.

## What changed

- **Quick guide** (viewer.js) — "go to Name faces... then go to Review
  people" → "go to People review"
- **Scan photos metrics** (scan-photos.js) — "Faces to name" → "Faces to
  review"; "People to review" metric now links to `/faces-review` instead
  of the old `/people-review` page
- **People Review completion button** (people-review.js) — "Name faces (N)"
  → "People review (N)"
- **User manual** (user-manual.md) — rewrote the two-stage workflow section
  into a single "People review" section with "When to stop" guidance
- **Old Review People page title** (photo_search.py) — "Review people" →
  "People review" in the browser tab
- **Code comment** (photo_search.py) — "Name-faces page" → "People-review
  page"

## Verified

- **Test suite**: 80 tests, all passed.
