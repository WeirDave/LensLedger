Fixed the vertical drag direction on the Photo Map globe to match how a physical globe behaves.

## Motivation

When dragging the globe vertically, the rotation was inverted relative to the physical-globe metaphor. The cursor uses a grab icon, which implies you're gripping the globe surface and pulling it. Dragging down should pull the top of the globe toward you, revealing the north pole — like pulling down on a real globe sitting in front of you. Instead, dragging down was revealing the south pole.

## Fix

Flipped the sign on the vertical drag delta in the `pointermove` handler: changed `drag.rx - dy * sensitivity` to `drag.rx + dy * sensitivity`. With screen coordinates where positive dy means the pointer moved downward, adding dy increases `rotX`, which moves the camera above the equator — looking down at the north pole.

## Verified

- Test suite: 101 tests passed
- Browser verification: drag down reveals north pole (Arctic, Greenland, northern Canada visible from above), drag up reveals south pole (Antarctica) — matches physical globe behavior
