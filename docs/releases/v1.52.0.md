# Filmstrip gets left and right scroll arrows

## Why this release

- The filmstrip in the photo viewer could only be moved by dragging it, using the scroll bar or the mouse wheel. There was no visible control at its edges for stepping through the thumbnails.

## What changed

### Photo viewer filmstrip

- A left arrow and a right arrow sit at the left and right edges of the filmstrip along the bottom of the viewer. Each click scrolls the strip by about four-fifths of its visible width.
- The left arrow is hidden when the strip is at its start. The right arrow is hidden at the end of the strip once every matching photo has loaded; while more photos remain to load, it stays visible.
- The arrows follow the light and dark themes and are not shown in the results grid.
- The Help page's Filmstrip section mentions the arrows.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 726 tests, all passing.
- Browser check of the filmstrip arrows in the light and dark themes, and of the results grid without them.
