## Why this release

The **Exact duplicates** view lists each set of copies side by side in the
filmstrip, but nothing marked where one set ended and the next began. With
several sets on screen, a pair of copies and the first half of the next pair
looked like a run of four, so choosing "the extras" meant guessing which
thumbnail belonged to which set.

## What changed

**Search scope → Exact duplicates**

- A coloured bar now sits between one set of copies and the next in the
  filmstrip.
- Each photo carries a small label in its top-left corner giving its place
  in its set, such as **1 of 2**. The likely original is always **1 of N**,
  so the copies to remove are the ones labelled 2 and above.
- The bars and labels stay correct as photos are trashed: after trashing a
  set's first photo, the one left is relabelled **1 of 1** and the bars are
  redrawn. They also extend as more of the list loads while scrolling.
- Readable in both the light and dark themes.

## Verified

- The duplicates test now also asserts each photo's set number, position and
  set size from the page's data feed.
- In Chromium against a synthetic library with three sets of copies: two
  bars between three sets, labels "1 of 2", "2 of 2" in each; trashing the
  first photo of a set left it labelled "1 of 1" with both bars still in
  place; no script errors. Checked in the light and dark themes.
