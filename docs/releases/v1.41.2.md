# Taken from and Taken until accept a year or a month

## Why this release

- The **Taken from** and **Taken until** boxes in the Filters panel only understood a complete date written as `YYYY-MM-DD`. Any other entry, such as a bare year, was discarded without a message, the box was emptied when the page reloaded, and the results were not filtered at all.

## What changed

### Filters panel

- **Taken from** and **Taken until** accept a full date (`2015-06-05`), a month (`2015-06`) or a year (`2015`).
- **Taken from** reads a year or month as its first day: `2015` means 2015-01-01 and `2015-06` means 2015-06-01.
- **Taken until** reads a year or month as its last day, so the whole period is included: `2015` means 2015-12-31 and `2015-02` means the last day of February. To see only photos from before 2015, enter `2014`.
- After **Apply filters**, the box shows the full date that was applied.
- Entries that are not a real date (for example `2015-13`) are still ignored.
- The Filters help text describes the accepted formats.

## Verified

- Full test suite: all passing.
- New tests cover year, month and day entries for both fields, invalid entries, and a catalog query for "Taken until" and "Taken from" with a bare year.
