# Drawn menu icons, collapsible side-panel sections

## Why this release

- The menu entries used emoji as icons, which look different on every system and could not follow the theme colours.
- The photo side panel always showed all four editing sections, so on a small window the one in use was often scrolled out of view.
- The "×" button on a tag chip was 16 pixels wide, which is hard to hit.

## What changed

### Menu (every page)

- Each menu entry, and the "About" button, now has a drawn line icon instead of an emoji. The icons use the text colour, so they follow the light and dark themes and brighten on hover and for the current page.
- When an update is available, the "Check for updates" entry keeps its icon and reads "Update available · vX.Y.Z".

### Photo viewer

- The headings "Primary subject", "Photo tags", "People in this photo", "Event / folder tags" and "Hidden tags" in the side panel are buttons. Selecting one collapses or expands its section.
- A collapsed section shows the number of items it holds next to its heading, for example "Photo tags · 3".
- The collapsed or expanded state of each section is remembered in the browser. All sections are expanded by default.
- The "×" button on a tag chip has a larger clickable area (about 28 pixels, and about 44 pixels on touch screens). The visible button is unchanged.

## Not changed

- The tag, subject, people and publish workflows, and the order of the fields in the side panel.

## Verified

- Full test suite: all passing, including a new check that the menu uses drawn icons and contains no emoji.
- Browser check of the menu in light and dark, and of the side panel on a desktop-width window and a phone-width window: sections collapse and expand, counts update, state survives a reload, and the headings are 44 pixels tall on a phone.
