## Why this release

After "Confirm all" is clicked in an "Also looks like …" panel on the People
page, LensLedger saves the confirmed faces and then searches the library for
further matches. That search can take several seconds on a large library, and
the only sign of it was a line of small text reading
"N confirmed. Looking for more…", with nothing moving.

## What changed

- A spinning camera-shutter icon, in the accent colour of the current theme,
  now appears in front of "Confirming N faces…" and
  "N confirmed. Looking for more…" in the "Also looks like …" panel. It stays
  until the next set of matches appears or the search finishes.
- Systems set to reduce motion get a much slower rotation.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 203 tests, all passing, on Python 3.12.10.
- Checked in the browser against a generated test library: the icon renders
  and rotates next to the status text in both the light and dark themes.
