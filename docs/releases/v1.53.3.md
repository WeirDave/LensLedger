# Photos brought in by Auto-import now get every scan

## Why this release

- The scan that follows an **Auto-import** ran photo locations, text recognition, meaning search and face detection, but had no photo tagging step of its own. New photos were tagged with what they show only when **Auto-classify photos after meaning search** was switched on. Installs that saved that setting as off left imported photos without automatic tags until **Run all scans** or **Classify photos** was used.
- With folder watching switched off, an import that finished while another scan was running was not scanned at all.

## What changed

### Scans after an import

- The scan that follows an **Auto-import**, whether from its timer or from **Run now**, runs the same scans as **Run all scans**, in the same order: photo locations, text recognition, meaning search, photo tagging and face detection. Each runs when photos are waiting for it. Meaning search, photo tagging and face detection require their optional setup.
- Scans started by folder watching follow the same rule.
- Photo tagging follows meaning search whenever new photos were indexed, whatever the **Auto-classify photos after meaning search** setting says. That setting, under **Settings**, **Metadata publishing**, applies to meaning search started on its own.

### Folder watching switched off

- When another scan is already running, the scan for imported photos is tried again every five minutes until it can run.
- Photos imported while that scan is running get a further scan after it finishes.

### User manual

- The **Auto-import** and **Settings** sections describe the scans that follow an import.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 736 tests, all passing, including new tests that an automatic scan runs every step in order, tags once, and is retried when another scan is running.
