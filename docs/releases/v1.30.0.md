# Publish bar stays visible, bigger photo area, phone drawer close button

## Why this release

- On desktop windows, **Preview & publish** and the status message sat below the visible part of the side panel and needed scrolling to reach.
- The side panel was a fixed 430 pixels wide and the filmstrip 158 pixels tall, which left the photo about 60% of a 1440 by 900 window and less in smaller ones.
- The photo details panel on a phone had no close button; closing it needed a tap on the 56 pixel strip beside it, or the Escape key.
- Phone browsers zoomed the page when a field in the side panel was tapped, because the text was 12 pixels.
- Some text and control colours were below the contrast needed for comfortable reading.

## What changed

### Side panel

- **Preview & publish**, **Restore last publish** and the status message are in a bar pinned to the bottom of the side panel. The "Publish to this photo" section above it keeps the photo description and the note about what will be changed. Status messages from saving a subject, tags or people appear in the same bar, below the buttons.
- The numbers 1 to 4 are removed from the headings "Primary subject", "Photo tags", "People in this photo" and "Event / folder tags". The sections can be used in any order.
- The publish section has a plain green border instead of a thick green left border.

### Photo area

- The side panel is 32% of the window width, between 320 and 430 pixels. At 1024 pixels wide it is 328 pixels instead of 430.
- The filmstrip is 134 pixels tall and its thumbnails are 150 by 108 pixels, instead of 158 and 170 by 132.
- At 1024 by 768 the photo area is 696 by 514 pixels, up from 594 by 490.

### Phones

- The photo details panel has a **Close details** bar at the top. Opening the panel moves focus to it, and closing the panel returns focus to the **Details** button.
- The button that opens the panel reads "Details" instead of an "i" symbol.
- Fields in the side panel and the toolbar use 16 pixel text and are 44 pixels tall on touch screens and in windows 768 pixels wide or narrower. The **Capture details** heading is 44 pixels tall there.
- The menu panel follows the visible screen height instead of the full window height.

### Colour and contrast

- The photo date and the status text use a darker brown in the light theme.
- **Trash this photo** uses a lighter red in the dark theme.
- Empty rating stars and the borders of fields, drop-downs and the date button in the viewer are darker in the light theme and lighter in the dark theme, so they can be seen on their backgrounds.
- The active item in the menu has a tinted background and a thin outline instead of a thick left border. This applies to the menu on every page.

## Not changed

- Publishing, trash, undo, search, the keyboard shortcuts and the order of the side panel sections.
- Pages other than the photo viewer, apart from the active menu item style.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 542 tests, all passing.
- Browser check in light and dark themes at 1024 by 768: **Preview & publish** and **Restore last publish** are visible without scrolling, the side panel is 328 pixels wide, the photo area is 696 by 514 pixels, the filmstrip is 134 pixels, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- At 375 by 812: the photo details panel opens at its full 319 pixel width with the **Close details** bar and the publish bar on screen, focus moves to the close bar on opening and back to the **Details** button on closing, the fields are 16 pixel text and 44 pixels tall, and the page does not scroll sideways.
