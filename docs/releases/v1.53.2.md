# Scanned photos are no longer dated by the day they were saved

## Why this release

- A photo with no capture date inside it and no date in its file or folder name was given the date from the image's "DateTime" field. That field holds the day the file was last saved, which editors rewrite and scanners set to the day of scanning. A scanned print was therefore listed under the year of the scan instead of the year the picture was taken, and appeared under the wrong year in the **Year** menu, in date filters and in the newest-first and oldest-first orders.

## What changed

### Photo dates

- The last-saved date is no longer used as a date taken. A photo's date now comes from, in order: the capture date stored in the image, then a date in the file name or folder name.
- A photo with none of these has no date. It is left out of the **Year** menu and date filters, and sorts after dated photos in newest-first order, instead of showing a wrong date.
- The next library scan re-reads the date of every photo that is already indexed, so photos that were given a last-saved date lose it. Photos with a stored capture date, or a date in their name, keep it.

## Not changed

- Auto-import still files a photo by its embedded time, and by the file's modified time when nothing else is found.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 730 tests, all passing, including an updated test that a last-saved date is not used as the date taken.
