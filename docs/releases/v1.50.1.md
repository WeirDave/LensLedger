# Version badge in the same place on every page

## Why this release

- The version badge was at the far right of the top bar on some pages and next to the LensLedger name on others, so its position changed from page to page.

## What changed

### Top bar

- The version badge is now the right-most item in the top bar on every page that shows it, after the light/dark theme button.
- Pages that moved the badge: the home page, **People** (including **Set-aside faces**), **Publish photos** and **Manage groups**.
- **Settings**, **Scan photos**, **Auto-import**, **Dev Tools** and the **User Manual** already had the badge in this position and are unchanged.
- The badge keeps its update behaviour: double-click to check for updates, single click to open the updates panel while a newer release is available.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 726 tests, all passing.
- Browser check of the top bar on the home page, People, Set-aside faces, Publish photos, Manage groups and Settings, in the dark theme and the light theme, and at a 700 px wide window.
