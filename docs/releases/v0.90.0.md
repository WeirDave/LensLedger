## Motivation

Assigning people to groups (Family, Friends, Coworkers, Band Mates, etc.) previously required clicking into each person's "Edit name" modal one at a time. With dozens or hundreds of people in a library, bulk group assignment was a major pain point.

## What changed

A new **Manage Groups** page at `/people/groups` provides a dedicated interface for bulk group operations:

- **Group sidebar** — lists all existing groups with live member counts. Click a group to select it as the target for add/remove operations. Create new groups inline with the text input at the top. Delete groups with the × button (hover to reveal).

- **People list** — shows every person in the library with their current group badges, aliases, and confirmed photo count. Search filters instantly by name, alias, or group name. Select individual people with checkboxes, or use "All" to select/deselect the visible set.

- **Bulk actions** — with a group selected and people checked, "Add to [group]" and "Remove from [group]" buttons perform the operation in one click. The page refreshes automatically to show updated badges and counts.

- **Navigation** — a "Manage groups" button was added to the People Index alpha bar, and the group manager page has a "← People Index" link back.

### New API endpoints

- `GET /api/people/all-with-groups` — returns all people with their aliases and group memberships in one call.
- `POST /api/groups/bulk-assign` — adds multiple people to a group at once, creating the group if it doesn't exist. Skips people who are already members.
- `POST /api/groups/bulk-remove` — removes multiple people from a group at once.

### Self-contained CSS

The page uses its own `group-manager.css` with all header, menu, and about-overlay styles included (following the same pattern as `people-review.css`), so it doesn't depend on `viewer.css`.
