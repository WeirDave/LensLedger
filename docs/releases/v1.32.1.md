# Trash buttons confirm inside the page

## Why this release

- The confirmation for moving photos to Trash used the browser's own dialog. A browser that has blocked further dialogs for a page answers such a dialog with Cancel and shows nothing, so **Trash this photo** appeared to do nothing.
- **Trash this photo** also returned without any message when the photo's details had not loaded.

## What changed

### Photo viewer

- **Trash this photo** (bottom of the side panel) opens a "Move to Trash?" panel with **Cancel** and **Move to Trash**. Closing the panel, pressing Escape or clicking outside it cancels.
- **Trash selected** (batch bar) uses the same panel.
- When the selected photo's details have not loaded, **Trash this photo** shows "This photo has not finished loading. Try again in a moment." in the status line under the side panel.
- When the selected photo is not in the filmstrip list, moving it to Trash no longer removes a different photo from the list.

## Not changed

- The Trash buttons on the People and Faces review pages still use the browser's confirmation dialog.
- Undo, the Trash & restore panel and where files are moved.

## Verified

- Full test suite: all passing, including new tests that check the viewer no longer uses the browser's confirmation for Trash and that the not-loaded message is present.
