Small clarity fix from live setup feedback: the text-scan message on the
setup page no longer leaves you guessing about what's safe to do next.

## Clearer OCR-scan messaging

After clicking "Scan for text now" during setup, the confirmation message
used to say only that scanning "started in the background" and could be
watched from Library health — leaving open two real questions: is it safe
to click "Open my library" right away, and is it safe to close the browser
tab?

Both messages (before and after starting the scan) now say plainly that
the scan runs on your computer, not in the browser tab, and keeps going
whether you move on to your library or close the tab. That matches how it
actually works: OCR runs as a background thread inside LensLedger's local
server process, which stays up independent of the browser tab pointed at
it.
