Same-day fix on 0.26.0's new "Name faces" page, from live feedback right
after it shipped: it was hard to find, and it was showing the same photo
over and over.

## One crowd photo, thirty cards

The report: "you have a whole bunch of duplicate photos, it's like it's
repeating the same photo over and over." Checked it against the real
library and it was real — the batch had 4 of its first 6 cards coming from
one "July 4th Boat and Fireworks" burst sequence.

The cause: `/api/faces/unidentified` ordered strictly by face id, newest
first, with no limit on how many faces could come from one photo. A
crowd photo can have dozens of detections in it — one Grand Prix crowd
shot in this library has 37 — and burst-mode sequences insert their faces
consecutively. Either one can dominate an entire batch with crops of
itself, leaving no room for anything else.

Fixed with a `ROW_NUMBER() ... PARTITION BY asset_id` window over the
candidate faces, capped to one per photo per batch. The photo's other
faces aren't lost — they surface on a later batch once this one gets
named or marked "Not a person." Verified against the real library: a
fresh batch of 30 now spans 30 distinct photos, versus one asset supplying
4 and several others supplying 2 before the fix.

## Buried where nobody would find it

The other report: "I had to hunt around in order to figure out where the
[expletive] it was." True — 0.26.0 added a header nav link to Scan your
photos, People review, and Photo map, but never to the main viewer, which
is the page most sessions actually start on. Added "🙂 Name faces (N)" to
the main hamburger menu, next to "Review people," with a live count of
how many faces are waiting.

## Verified

- Full automated suite (64 tests) green.
- Manually verified against the real 50,909-file library: the main
  viewer's hamburger menu now shows "🙂 Name faces (4,062)"; a fresh
  `/faces-review` batch of 30 spans 30 distinct source photos with zero
  repeats, confirmed by asset-id count in both a direct database query
  and the live page.
