# Side panel shows what a photo has and what is missing

## Why this release

- The side panel next to a photo was mostly explanatory text. Which tags, people and subject the photo already had, and what was still empty, took reading to work out. The Trash button was at the bottom, below everything else.

## What changed

### Photo viewer side panel

- The paragraph under each section heading is now one line.
- The "Metadata for this photo" block at the top is removed. A line above **Preview & publish** at the bottom of the panel says changes stay in LensLedger until they are published.
- **Primary subject**, **Photo tags**, **People in this photo** and **Event / folder tags** list what the photo already has. A section with nothing in it shows **None yet**. The box for adding more is directly underneath.
- The photo description is its own **Description** section, in the same style as the others, instead of inside the green "Publish to this photo" box. The sections collapse like the others and remember their state.
- **Trash this photo** is the **Trash** button beside the file name, date and folder at the top of the panel. It asks for confirmation as before, and on a phone it is in the same place when **Details** is open.
- **Capture details** and **Hidden tags** keep their place below the editing sections.

## Verified

- Full test suite: 726 tests, all passing.
- Browser check of the side panel in the light and dark themes, on a wide window and at phone width.
